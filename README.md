📊 Customer Churn Analysis Summary
Overall Churn Rate:

About 26.5% of customers have churned, as shown in the pie chart. This highlights a significant churn issue for the business.

Demographics and Churn:

Senior Citizens are more likely to churn than younger customers, with a visibly higher churn rate in that group.

Gender does not show a strong difference in churn, indicating other factors may play a larger role.

Tenure Impact:

Customers with a short tenure (1–2 months) are much more likely to churn.

Long-term users show higher retention, suggesting satisfaction improves with extended service.

Contract Type:

Customers on month-to-month contracts churn the most.

One-year and two-year contracts show far better retention, likely due to commitment and discounts.

Service Usage & Churn:

Customers not using certain services like OnlineSecurity, OnlineBackup, TechSupport, or StreamingTV exhibit higher churn.

In contrast, those actively using these services are more likely to stay, emphasizing the importance of bundled services and added value.

Visual Insights:

Countplots and stacked bar charts throughout the notebook visually reinforce the above patterns.

The stacked percentage bar for Senior Citizens clearly illustrates the skew toward churn in that group.
